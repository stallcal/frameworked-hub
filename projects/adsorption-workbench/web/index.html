Converted uptake

Follow the units

All mass-normalized values refer to dry adsorbent mass. “g/g” means grams adsorbate per gram adsorbent; it is not the mass fraction of the loaded sample.

Display rounded to seven significant digits for readability. This is not an uncertainty estimate.

Pressure converter

Unit conversion only; a gauge pressure does not become an absolute pressure by changing units.
Assumptions and definitions
  • 1 mmol/g = 1 mol/kg. Mass conversion uses the adsorbate molar mass.
  • Gas-volume uptake uses the ideal-gas equation, Vₘ = RT/P, with Z = 1. R = 8.31446261815324 kPa L mol⁻¹ K⁻¹. This is a reference-volume convention; it does not assert ideal behavior under experimental conditions or phase stability at the reference state.
  • “STP” is ambiguous without temperature and pressure. The two presets intentionally produce different molar volumes.
  • Excess and absolute adsorption are preserved as labels. The tool does not convert one to the other or change dry/wet mass normalization.
  • No mixture, real-gas correction, uncertainty propagation, or isotherm fitting is included.

References: IUPAC Gold Book — standard conditions for gases; NIST — molar gas constant.
