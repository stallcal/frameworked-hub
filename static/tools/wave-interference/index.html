How does the phase difference between two waves affect interference?

These waves have the same frequency and wavelength. Their amplitudes add at each point: equal waves reinforce at 0° and cancel at 180°.

For amplitudes A₁ and A₂, the resultant amplitude is R = √(A₁² + A₂² + 2A₁A₂ cos φ). At 180°, R = |A₁ − A₂|: unequal waves do not cancel completely.

An extra path length ΔL produces a phase difference φ = 360° × ΔL/λ (modulo 360°). A full wavelength restores the same phase; half a wavelength reverses it.
