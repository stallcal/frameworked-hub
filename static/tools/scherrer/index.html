THE EQUATION
D = Kλβ cos θ
β = FWHM in radians · θ = Bragg angle

Research beta. The calculator estimates coherent-domain length, not particle diameter. Without measured instrumental broadening, results are uncorrected and should be treated as preliminary. Files stay in your browser.
