UNDER THE HOOD / THE CHEMISTRY

One basis. Any proportion.

The calculation first converts the known quantity into millimoles. It then multiplies that amount by the ratio of molar equivalents and converts the result back to mass.

ni=nknown×rirknown
mi=ni×Mi

n is amount in mmol, r is relative molar equivalents, and M is molar mass in g/mol. Numerically, g/mol = mg/mmol, so m is in mg.

Limitations and assumptions

Choosing a molar ratio does not establish a balanced chemical equation, limiting reagent, expected product composition, theoretical yield, or synthesis outcome. For salts, confirm the hydrate or solvate form. Purity corrections, stock solutions, and reagent lookup are not included in this version. Independent experimental verification is needed before using any quantities as part of a synthesis procedure.
