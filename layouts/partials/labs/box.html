The model, explained.

Eₙ = n²h² / (8mₑL²)
ψₙ(x) = √(2/L) sin(nπx/L)

Here L is the box width, mₑ is the electron mass, h is Planck’s constant, and n is a positive integer. The probability in an interval is the area under |ψ|² within it. A negative wavefunction is allowed; probability density is always nonnegative.

This is one electron in a one-dimensional infinite well, with zero potential inside and impenetrable walls. It is a stationary energy state, so its probability density does not move. Real molecules have finite, more complicated potentials. Width changes compare separate wells, not a time-dependent compression.
