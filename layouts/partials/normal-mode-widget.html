WHY IR / RAMAN?

IR: an allowed fundamental has a nonzero dipole derivative, ∂μ/∂Q.

Raman: an allowed fundamental has a nonzero polarizability derivative, ∂α/∂Q.

For a centrosymmetric molecule, ordinary IR and Raman fundamentals obey mutual exclusion.
