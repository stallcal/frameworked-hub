Frameworked is a growing collection of lessons, articles, and research notes about metal–organic frameworks and related materials. It is for chemistry students, researchers, and anyone curious about the field.

## Behind the site
I’m Caleb Stallard, part of the [Wriedt Lab at UT Dallas](https://labs.utdallas.edu/wriedt/). Frameworked brings together my MOF research and science communication interests. Over time, it will also become a home for video essays and resources from my research.

MOFs are the starting point because they are central to my research. My longer-term aim is to explore other areas of chemistry and, when the questions lead there, the wider STEM world. The common thread is curiosity about how things work, starting with their building blocks.

## Following the evidence
Scientific explanations link back to original research where possible. Sources and image credits stay close to the material they support, and open questions are distinguished from reported findings.
