## Begin with a question
For a future essay about MOF-5, a useful starting question is: how do the authors connect a proposed structure with evidence for a porous material? Keep the original paper open alongside the explanation. {{< cite "1" >}}

{{< editor-note >}}
Replace this introduction with your own hook: a question, an unexpected observation, or something that caught your attention while reading. Identify the paper and explain why you chose it.

## Follow the evidence
{{< editor-note >}}
Choose one figure or measurement. Explain what was measured, under which conditions, and how it supports the authors’ claim. Use an original illustration or a figure with appropriate reuse permission and attribution.
{{< /editor-note >}}

## Make room for uncertainty
{{< editor-note >}}
Separate the authors’ findings from your interpretation. Note limitations, competing explanations, and questions the experiment does not answer. Avoid generalizing one material’s behavior to every MOF.
{{< /editor-note >}}
