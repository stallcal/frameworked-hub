- **Science Resources channel avatars (v0.1.4):** The previous Unavatar endpoint was unsuitable for a page with 37 channels because its public tier has a low daily request limit. YouTube handles now resolve through [avatarsapi.com](https://avatarsapi.com/docs) (`/youtube/@handle?size=112`), and the two channels with documented stable YouTube `UC...` channel IDs use [banner.yt](https://banner.yt/api-docs) (`/{channelId}?type=avatar&width=112&height=112`). Images are loaded lazily and shown only after successful loading; initials remain as fallbacks. Providers are independent of Frameworked and YouTube, and thumbnails remain owned by their respective creators. No channel image bytes are hosted here. The catalog page provides visible provider attribution. **Operational limitation:** the free avatarapi tier is also rate-limited (50 fresh lookups per IP per day; cached hits do not count); a future stable fix is to locally cache verified images under appropriate terms. As with any external service, image availability and identity must be visually confirmed on the deployed page.

## High-resolution logo activation

Upload the verified transparent 1024 × 1024 PNG as `assets/images/frameworked-logo-hd.png` and commit it to `main`. The Hugo site automatically uses this image for the homepage and derives the header PNG, favicon and Apple touch icon from it. The existing transparent assets remain fallbacks until the higher-resolution file is present. This image must have actual alpha transparency (RGBA or PNG `tRNS`), not an opaque white background.
